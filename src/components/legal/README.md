# Legal & Compliance Components

This directory houses components and layout templates for the legal & compliance pages defined in the PDFs:
- Privacy Policy (`Privacy-policy_ XSW_UI.pdf`)
- Terms & Conditions (`Terms-Conditions_ XSW_UI.pdf`)
- Refund Policy (`Refund-policy_ XSW_UI.pdf`)
- Cookie Policy (`Cookie-policy_ XSW_UI.pdf`)

Each page shares a structured content layout with clear typography, table of contents navigation, and compliant corporate policy formatting.
